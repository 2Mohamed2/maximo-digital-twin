Energy balance of the last 15-minute interval

Check meter energy against the customer meters plus the technical loss the twin computes from the measured currents. What is left is the unexplained (non-technical) loss. The axis starts at 80 % of the feeder energy.

Unexplained loss per phase

Each phase against a third of the feeder energy, by the phase the network model records. A wrong phase gives a gain on one phase and a loss on another; theft gives a loss on one.

Voltage from the busbar to the end of the feeder

Measured phase voltages at the busbar and at the end-of-feeder meter (cabinet D), the end voltage the twin expects from the load flow, and the EN 50160 band 207-253 V.
