The legal window: temperature and time

Every particle must stay at 72 °C or more for 15 s or more (PMO, EU 853/2004). Green: legal. Blue dot: the milk now (fastest particle, lower of the two holding tube thermometers); grey cross: the design point.

Holding time against flow

Fastest particle time = 3600 × η × V / Q for the 49 l holding tube. The flow control must never pass the capacity at which the tube still gives 15 s.

Regeneration against fouling

Balanced counterflow: regeneration = NTU / (1 + NTU), with NTU falling as the plates foul (U / Uclean). At today's flow. Every lost point of regeneration is paid in steam and ice water.
